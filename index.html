Pincoin - Privacy Policy & Terms Pincoin is an experimental entertainment platform connected to the Pincoin memecoin. 1) Wallet use: The wallet entered is used only to verify ownership of the Pincoin token (read-only). Seed phrases/private keys are never requested or stored. The full wallet address will be saved only if the user wins, to enable manual payout. 2) Data: Failed attempts are NOT stored. Only winners (full wallet, winning pin, timestamp) are saved to allow manual payout. 3) Prizes: Pool: 10,000,000 PIN. Max prize per event: up to 25% of the pool (decided by admin/community). 4) Probabilities: Win probability is very low and can be adjusted. Max 20% if the token reaches $1 USD. 5) Payout: Prize is sent manually to the winner's wallet. It may take 20–30 days depending on liquidity. 6) Acceptance: By accepting, the user gives permission for the checks described. 7) Liability: Pincoin is not responsible for losses or misuse.
